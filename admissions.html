PARENT'S CHECKLIST

What to Look
For in a School

Choosing the right school is one of the most important decisions for your child's future. Here are the key factors every parent should consider.

Start Exploring

Curriculum Type

Check if the school offers Cambridge (O/A Levels), Matric, IB or other boards that suit your child's goals.

Location & Transport

Consider distance from home, availability of school transport and safety of the route.

Facilities

Look for labs, library, sports grounds, computer rooms, art studios and medical facilities.

Fee Structure

Compare monthly fees, admission charges, annual fees and any hidden costs before deciding.

Teacher Quality

Qualified, experienced teachers with low student-to-teacher ratios make a huge difference.

Reputation & Results

Research the school's academic results, alumni success stories and overall reputation in the community.

BE PREPARED

General Documents
Most Schools Require

These are the most commonly requested documents. Always confirm with the specific school as requirements may vary.

Birth Certificate

Original + photocopy of the child's B-Form or birth certificate issued by NADRA.

Previous Result Card

Last year's report card or leaving certificate from the previous school.

Passport Size Photos

Usually 4–6 recent passport size photographs of the student.

Medical / Vaccination Record

Vaccination history or a basic medical fitness certificate may be required.

Parent's CNIC Copy

Photocopy of both parents' CNICs for verification and contact purposes.

Filled Application Form

The school's own admission form, filled completely and signed by a parent or guardian.
